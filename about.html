about us

Your trusted engineering partner in Lira City

Albrighton Engineering Company Limited is a Ugandan-based multi-disciplinary engineering firm committed to delivering high-quality and innovative solutions across various engineering sectors. We aim to shape a sustainable future through quality workmanship and trusted partnership.

We position ourselves as a one-stop hub for comprehensive engineering and development solutions — covering electrical works, metal fabrication & welding, carpentry, aluminium & glass, water engineering, plumbing, general construction and renovation.

From our base in Rainbow, near Lipton, Aduku Road, Lira City, we serve clients across Northern Uganda with practical, durable and professionally executed work.
